RoomMitra – Privacy Policy / गोपनीयता नीति

Last updated: 24 October 2026

RoomMitra ("we", "app") connects room/flat owners directly with tenants. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

  • Account: mobile number, name, and optionally email and city/address that you enter.
  • Listings: property details, photos and the contact number you choose to publish.
  • Usage inside the app: saved favourites, chat messages and settings.

How it is stored and used

In the current version, all of this data is stored only on your own device (app storage). It is used only to run the app's features. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. The contact number you put on a listing is shown to people viewing that listing.

Permissions

The app opens your phone's dialer, WhatsApp, maps and email apps only when you tap those buttons. Photos are read only when you choose them for a listing or profile.

Deleting your data

Profile → सुरक्षा और गोपनीयता → "मेरा डेटा मिटाएं" removes all app data from your device. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

Children

The app is meant for users aged 18 and above.
