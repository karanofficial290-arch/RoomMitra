RoomMitra – Privacy Policy / गोपनीयता नीति

Last updated: 24 October 2026 (v2 – online version)

RoomMitra ("we", "app") connects room/flat owners directly with tenants. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

  • Account: your email address (used for one-time-code login), and the name, mobile number and city you choose to add.
  • Listings: property details, photos, and the contact name/number you publish. These are visible to everyone who uses the app.
  • Activity: saved favourites, chat messages between tenants and owners, reports you submit, and listing view counts.
  • Profile photo if you upload one, and an optional short bio.
  • Location: owners may pin their property on a map (the exact pin and full address are private; other users only see the area and an approximate location of about 350 m). Your phone's GPS is used only when you tap "मेरे पास के कमरे" or the GPS button, to show nearby rooms — it is not stored on our servers.
  • Listing videos if an owner uploads a room video.

How it is stored and used

Data is stored on secure servers operated by Supabase (Mumbai, India region), protected by row-level security so that only you (and, for chats, the other participant) can access private data. We use it only to run RoomMitra: showing listings, letting people contact owners, and preventing fraud. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Permissions

Internet access is needed to load listings and messages. Location permission is optional and only requested when you use GPS features. The app opens your phone's dialer, WhatsApp, maps and email apps only when you tap those buttons. Photos are read only when you choose them.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → "अकाउंट डिलीट करें". Your listings, photos, favourites and chats are removed immediately and your login is permanently erased within 7 days. You can also request deletion by emailing us from your registered email address at roommitara@gmail.com.

Children

The app is meant for users aged 18 and above.
